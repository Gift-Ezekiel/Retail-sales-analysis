## Key Findings

### 1. Overall Performance

The business generated approximately **$1.06 million in revenue**, **$359,000 in profit**, and a **34.0% profit margin**, with **22,799 units sold**.

Overall performance improved in 2025 compared with 2024.

### 2. Seasonality

- Sales consistently peak in November and December.
- January and February record the lowest sales in both years.
- This pattern is consistent with increased holiday shopping activity, although the available data cannot establish the cause.

### 3. Product Performance

- **Laptop Pro 15:** Top product by revenue and profit, but has a relatively thin profit margin of approximately 16%.
- **Coffee Beans 1kg:** Best-selling product by units, with over 1,600 units sold.
- **Smart Watch S:** Records a healthy profit margin of approximately 34%, the highest among Electronics products.

### 4. Category Performance

| Category | Revenue (Approx.) | Profit Margin (Approx.) | Key Finding |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Electronics | $560K | 23% | Highest revenue but weakest margin, partly reflecting its cost structure. |
| Furniture | $174K | 31% | Heavy discounting (approximately 20% on average) puts pressure on profitability. |
| Groceries | $121K | 49% | Highest unit volume, supported by relatively small discounts. |
| Clothing | $108K | 63% | Highest profit margin among all categories. |
| Home and Kitchen | $92K | 53% | Lowest revenue but maintains a strong profit margin. |

### 5. Customer Performance

- **Noah Hill:** Highest customer by both revenue and profit.
- **Ruth Taylor:** Records the highest number of orders and receives higher average discounts than other top customers.
- **Sarah Johnson:** Has relatively few orders but makes large purchases, presenting a potential customer retention and engagement opportunity.

### 6. Store and Regional Performance

- **Downtown Flagship (East):** Highest-performing store.
- **Greenfield Outlet (South):** Weakest-performing store.
- Profit margins are relatively similar across regions, ranging from approximately 33% to 35%. This suggests that location has a greater impact on revenue than overall profitability.
- **Lakeview Center:** The only store recording declining revenue year over year.
- Several stores experienced a decline of a few percentage points in profit margin in 2025, despite relatively unchanged average discounts. This suggests that changes in product mix may have contributed to the decline.

### 7. Shipping Performance

- Average shipping time is approximately 3 days, with a range of 1–7 days.
- Shipping times show no meaningful differences across stores, regions, or months.
- Shipping performance does not appear to explain the weaker performance of certain stores.

### 8. Discount Analysis

- Within individual product categories, higher discount levels did not consistently translate into higher unit sales.
- In Furniture, profit margin declines from approximately 44% with no discount to approximately 16% when discounts exceed 20%.
- This indicates that aggressive discounting can significantly reduce profitability without a corresponding increase in sales volume.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Control discounting, starting with Furniture.** Higher discounts do not appear to generate additional sales volume. Introduce discount limits and test them in selected stores before implementing them across the business.

2. **Protect Electronics profitability.** Review pricing and discount policies, particularly for laptops, where even small discounts can significantly reduce already-thin profit margins.

3. **Increase promotion of Clothing and Home and Kitchen.** These categories have relatively strong profit margins and potential for revenue growth.

4. **Prepare for seasonal demand.** Increase inventory planning and staffing ahead of the November–December sales peak while adjusting expectations for the quieter January–February period.

5. **Investigate underperforming stores.** Examine Greenfield Outlet and Lakeview Center to identify possible issues involving store size, local customer traffic, and product mix.

6. **Review customer discount patterns.** Evaluate discounts by customer segment and product mix before making changes to individual customer pricing or discount arrangements.

### Conclusion

The analysis shows that strong revenue does not always translate into strong profitability. Electronics generates the highest revenue but has a relatively weak margin, while Clothing and Home and Kitchen deliver stronger margins at lower revenue levels.

The biggest opportunities lie in controlling discount-related margin losses, improving underperforming stores, protecting high-margin products, and planning around seasonal demand.
